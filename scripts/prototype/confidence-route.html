Confidence Route: merge, group, or leave alone

The question: if we score every pair of listings that share a start time, and route them by a confidence band — Merge the near-certain ones, Group the "looks like the same event" ones without deleting anything, and leave the rest Separate — does it get the known pairs right, and can we make it never silently merge two listings that are actually different events?

Domain language: a listing is one row from one source. Merge means one row survives and the other source is folded in (destructive — the thing we are most afraid of). Group means both rows stay in the database, but every consumer renders them as one card, with every listing still visible. The whole point of the prototype is to feel where the line between the two should sit, and how hard the start-time and location guards have to work.

Invariant worth checking as you click: the threshold and every guard only move the Group band. The Merge band is driven entirely by "identical normalised title, same instant, compatible location" and ignores all of them. Deletions can only ever come from that narrow, exact path.
